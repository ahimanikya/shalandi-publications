A soulful poetry collection

By Dr. Hema Savithri Jan 16, 2026

 Rendezvous with Dreams, a soulful poetry collection by Pradeep Biswal, retired IAS officer and one of Odisha's most endearing bilingual voices, is a kind of book that does not merely speak to the reader but 'for' them. Woven with nearly four decades of lived experience, it moves freely through philosophy, nature, love, grief, and the full breadth of human emotion. He brings before us every human feeling with an ease that feels effortless, yet clearly it's not, capturing in plain language the thing most writers spend a lifetime straining toward.

The collection's governing spirit is best understood through its most resonant lines:

" Life is too short

And slippery

To measure the distance

Between the passing moments."

These lines are characteristic of Biswal's style which is direct, unadorned, and carrying a weight that lands squarely in the chest. Time, memory and impermanence run through the collection like a quiet current, surfacing in meditations on pandemic, on loss and grief, on love and longing, on the strange intimacy that emerges when ordinary life is stripped away. His natural world ( rain - soaked earth, rivers, and seasons) participates in mirroring the inner states of a speaker who has paid close attention to both the world within and without.

Biswal writes in free verse and his style is simple, direct and unhurried. Rendezvous with Dreams, is a mirror held up to the reader with such clarity and care that the reflection looking back at them is unmistakably theirs.
