Policy & Terms

Thank you for trusting and shopping at shalandi.in. Please read the policies below carefully to ensure your rights.

1. Return and Exchange Policy

shalandi.in is committed to providing quality books. However, if there are any issues, we are ready to support returns and exchanges under the following conditions:

Conditions for Return/Exchange:

  • Product has printing defects from the manufacturer (missing pages, faded print, inverted pages...).
  • Product is damaged during shipping (torn, wet, deformed).
  • Wrong product or wrong version delivered compared to the order placed.

Applicable period:

Please notify us within **02 days** from the date of receiving the Books.

Return Process:

  1. Contact us via email or hotline, provide the order code and image/video evidence of the product's condition.
  2. After confirmation, we will guide you on how to return the item.
  3. shalandi.in will send a new product or issue a 100% refund of the product value (excluding initial shipping fees) within 3-5 working days after receiving the returned goods.

**Note:** shalandi.in does not accept returns or exchanges due to reasons like "dislike" or "change of mind." The returned product must be intact and unused.

2. Information Privacy Policy

shalandi.in respects and is committed to protecting the personal information of its customers.

Purpose of Information Collection:

  • Processing and delivering orders.
  • Customer support and complaint resolution.
  • Providing information about promotions and new books (when the customer agrees to receive updates).

Scope of Collection:

We collect the following information: Full name, shipping address, phone number, email, and purchase history.

Confidentiality Commitment:

  • We do not sell, trade, or share customers' personal information with any third party, except for shipping partners to facilitate delivery or when required by legal authorities.
  • Your data is protected by advanced technical and security measures.

3. Shipping Policy

Estimated Delivery Time:

  • **In and around Bhubaneswar within 48 hours.
  • **Other cities:** 3-10 working days.

Delivery time may change due to objective factors (weather, epidemics, special events...). We will notify you if there is a significant delay.

Shipping Fees:

Shipping fees are calculated automatically on the checkout page based on the delivery address and order weight. We frequently offer **free shipping** programs for orders that meet a minimum value of Rs.500/- and above; please check the homepage for details.

Disclaimer:

shalandi.in is not liable for any damages arising from the misuse of the website or books. We reserve the right to change and update these policies and terms without prior notice.

5. Payment Policy

We offer secure and convenient payment methods:

  • **Pay Later: We take up directly with the Custmer for payment method.
