About Publication

Books will be published under Print on Demand Scheme.
Banner- Shalandi Books.
ISBN number under Publishers name.
Copy rights with the Writer.
The Author has to sign an agreement with the Publisher before publication.

The Author has to bear the incidental expenses, as under;
DTP of the Book
Proof Correction
Page setting
Cover Design
Inner Illustrations (if any)

Books will be available at online shopping platforms
shalandi.in
amazon.in
flipkart.com
At Bookfairs in Bhubaneswar.

About Authors Copy & Royality

Author will get five complimentary copies.
He can purchase more books with 30% discount directly from the publisher.
However 15 days time required for bulk purchases.
The Author will be paid royality @ 10% on books sold by the Publisher
(except books purchased by the Author, as mentioned in the above clause).

Quality control of Books

We publish all type of Books.
Quality Literature Books are our first priority.
Manuscript submitted for publication will be evaluated by an Expert Committee.
For translated Book, permission from the original writer required.
