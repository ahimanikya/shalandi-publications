Where every page opens up a new world.

Our Mission

shalandi.in is an online bookshop to sell books throughout India. We sell mostly Odia books or books from Odia publishers. We deal with all types of literature books including Short stories, Novels, Poetry books etc. Our aim is to bind the gap between people looking for Odia literature books and the local publishers who has no online outlet to sell their books. We are giving maximum discounts on books to cater the needs of literature loving people. We strive to deliver books with best quality and efficient shipping methods.

We don't just sell books; we curate and introduce worthwhile stories, valuable knowledge, and premium reading experiences for modern readers.
